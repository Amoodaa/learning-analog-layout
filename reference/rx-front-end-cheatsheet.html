Rules of thumb

  • The central tension: ESD wants wide metal and big clamps; bandwidth wants minimum C on the same node.
  • 56 Gb/s NRZ and 112 Gb/s PAM4 share a 28 GHz Nyquist, and so share the same pad-node C budget.
  • Any fF you add at the pad node also shows up as return loss. That's why T-coils exist (Galal & Razavi, JSSC 2003).
